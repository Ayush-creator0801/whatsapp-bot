BRIGHT PATH ACADEMY (demo business, all details are fictional)

ABOUT
Bright Path Academy is a coaching institute for school and entrance-exam students.
Established in 2014. Located in a rented floor above a bookstore, near the main market.

COURSES AND FEES (per academic year)
- Class 9 and 10 (Maths + Science): Rs 24,000
- Class 11 and 12 Science (Physics, Chemistry, Maths/Biology): Rs 48,000
- JEE Main foundation (Class 11-12): Rs 65,000
- NEET foundation (Class 11-12): Rs 65,000
Fees can be paid in two instalments: 60% at admission and 40% by 31 October.
Registration fee of Rs 1,000 is charged once at admission and is not refundable.
Study material is included in the fee.

BATCH TIMINGS
- Class 9 and 10: Monday to Saturday, 4:00 PM to 6:00 PM
- Class 11 and 12 Science: Monday to Saturday, 6:30 AM to 9:00 AM
- JEE and NEET foundation: Monday to Saturday, 5:00 PM to 8:00 PM
- Sunday: weekly test for all batches, 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM
Each batch has a maximum of 30 students.

ADMISSION
- Admissions open from 1 March every year. New batches start on 1 April.
- A free demo class is available. Book by calling or messaging the office.
- Documents needed: previous year's marksheet, one passport photo, parent's phone number.
- A short entry test (30 minutes) is taken for JEE and NEET foundation batches only.
